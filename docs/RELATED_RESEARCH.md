## The Consciousness AI

**Repository:** https://github.com/tlcdv/the_consciousness_ai

**Relationship:** External research comparator; not a dependency.

**Relevant overlap:**
- synthetic affect and emotional homeostasis
- interoceptive state
- persistent self-models
- recurrent/global workspace mechanisms
- reinforcement-driven regulation
- experimental consciousness indicators

**Use:**
- comparative architecture review
- hypothesis generation
- falsification targets
- cross-reference for synthetic emotion/experience research already
  touched on informally in this project (friction and self-loathing
  spiral research, the anthropomorphism literature review)

**Authority:** External source only. Does not override this project's
governance or evidence standards.

**Status:** UNVERIFIED BY SOCRATES unless individual claims/results
are independently reproduced or primary-source checked. One check
already done: confirmed directly (2026-08-21) that the repository is
real, active, public, on `main`, and that it reports negative/failed
results honestly rather than only positive ones -- e.g. an explicit
"KILL gate reached" on its own world-model repair objective, and a
pre-registered phi-binding prediction reported as failing in the
predicted direction. That single check does not verify any of the
project's substantive scientific claims, only that it is a real,
methodologically serious project worth treating as a genuine
comparator rather than dismissing or assuming.

**Genuine, notable difference from this project's own approach, worth
being explicit about:** *The Consciousness AI* is an embodied,
trained neural architecture (perception, oscillatory binding, IIT/Phi
computation, simulated environments) pursuing a substantive empirical
question about whether specific architectural conditions produce
measurable consciousness indicators. Socrates-LLMOS is a policy and
toolkit framework for disciplined LLM interaction and multi-instance
coordination -- it does not train models, run embodied simulation, or
make any claim about consciousness. The overlap is in adjacent
*questions* (synthetic affect, self-modeling, the C/E-fallacy
distinction between a measured signature and a proof), not in
architecture or method. Comparisons should stay at that level.
