# Kernel Adoption Tracking

**Not automated.** Same as `EXECUTION_BOUNDARY_UPDATES.md` — this only
grows when someone actually checks and logs something. First version
built from real ledger entries already on record, not a blank
template, but still manually curated, not monitored.

## Category 1: Kernel rules, by whether they have logged evidence of actual use

"Demonstrated" means a real, ledger-traceable instance of the rule
actually being applied — not that it's important or correct. Absence
from the demonstrated list is not a defect in the rule; most kernel
sections are broad principles that don't leave a discrete trace every
time they shape a response.
